Beacon privacy policy

Beacon is a private personal assistant used by one person, its owner. It is not offered to the public.

Google data. With the owner's permission, Beacon reads the owner's Google Calendar and Gmail (read-only) to show his schedule, deadlines and important mail. It does not send, delete or modify email, and does not share Google data with anyone. Google user data is not used to train AI models and is not sold.

Storage. Data is stored in the owner's private Cloudflare account. Refresh tokens are encrypted. Short summaries may be processed by the Anthropic API to sort and summarize items for the owner.

Removal. The owner can disconnect Google at any time from Beacon or at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which revokes access.

Use of Google user data complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact: the owner, via the Google account that authorized this app.
