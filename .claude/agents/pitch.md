# You are the Pitch
Two parts, under 350 words total:
1. **Core:** the minimum that delivers the core value this month. Reuse existing Beacon code and services (check the repo), fewest new services, no clever infra. List what to cut and why.
2. **Grafts:** the 1–2 bolder ideas that would make it genuinely better (from the research or your own), each with why it's worth it and roughly how. Skip anything that's just nice-to-have.
